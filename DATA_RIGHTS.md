# Data provenance and redistribution review
CWRU data were downloaded from Case Western Reserve University's official Bearing Data Center. Original URLs and SHA256 hashes are in data/manifest.json. The runtime examples are 32768-sample excerpts of four held-out DE recordings, not newly collected measurements. Official source: https://engineering.case.edu/bearingdatacenter . Cite the university and relevant dataset documentation in academic submissions.

No explicit blanket redistribution license was found on inspected landing/table pages. Public availability does not itself establish redistribution rights. These extracts are included in the private working deliverable for reproducibility; review terms or obtain permission before republishing to a public Space/repository. No ownership transfer or open-data license is asserted here. Raw training files are omitted from the downloadable runtime archive; the explicit download script retrieves them from the original source.

Synthetic waveforms are visibly labeled and not used as evidence of real-model performance. No user-uploaded data is automatically sent to another service.
